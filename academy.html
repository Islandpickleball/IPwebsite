Island Pickleball Academy

Level up their game

San Juan Island's first dedicated youth pickleball program — structured instruction, athletic development, and high-energy fun for kids ages 10 and up.

Why this program

A positive, engaging pathway

For too long, local youth options have lacked structured court instruction. We're changing that — building a place where kids learn teamwork, hand-eye coordination, and a sport they can play for life.

Every session combines expert coaching with a gamified progression model, so kids stay motivated and excited to come back each week. And every session ends the same way it starts: with our motto — play hard, be kind.

The details

  • Ages: 10 and up, all skill levels welcome
  • Format: 4-week cohort, one session per week
  • Where: San Juan Island Fairgrounds indoor facility, Friday Harbor
  • Class size: strictly capped at 16 players — two groups of eight for focused instruction
  • Coach: David Kane, PPR Certified & annually background-checked

The skill ladder

Earn your bands on the court

Four weeks, four milestones. Each wristband is earned by demonstrating the skill — never by attendance, never by purchase.

Week 1

The Foundation

Grip, athletic stance, paddle-to-ball control, and spatial awareness. Milestone: 5 consecutive drop-and-catches and safe, controlled rallying.

Week 2

The Kitchen Control

Patience, non-volley-zone rules, and soft-touch placement. Milestone: a 10-shot cooperative cross-court dink rally.

Week 3

Serves & Transitions

A consistent underhand serve, deep returns, and moving forward to the kitchen line. Milestone: a reliable legal serve and deep return.

Week 4

Match Play & Graduation

Scoring, strategy, and sportsmanship in the Island Pickleball Cup — our end-of-series round robin. Milestone: full participation and graduation.

Our culture

Play hard. Be kind.

Give your best

Move your feet, pay attention, try difficult things, and keep working after mistakes.

Stay composed

Feel your feelings — then breathe, reset, and choose your next action. Breathe. Reset. Next ball.

Speak with kindness

Encourage partners. Respect opponents. No mocking, taunting, or blame — ever.

Play with integrity

Be honest, take responsibility, and care more about a fair game than an easy point.

Lift the court

Include new players, recognize good play, and leave every court better than you found it.

Belong, first

Every player receives the Island Pickleball crew band at their first session — a symbol of belonging, not of skill. Skill bands are earned later, on the court.

For parents & families

Their game. Our example.

We want enthusiastic parents — cheer, smile, and enjoy watching your kids play. What we don't allow is pressure from the sidelines: no shouted corrections, no criticism, no celebrating another child's mistake.

Every registering parent or guardian reads and signs our Parent & Spectator Code of Conduct before their child's registration is complete. It's how we protect the joy of the game for every kid on court.

This is their game. Our responsibility is to help them love it.
