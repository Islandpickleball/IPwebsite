Events we run

From island club events to benefit tournaments benefiting United Way of San Juan County, our events are known for being organized, welcoming, and competitive in equal measure.

Results and photo galleries from past events live here — podium shots, bracket boards, and the people who make island pickleball what it is.

Why play ours?

  • Proper brackets and divisions — organized down to the crossover matches
  • A welcoming first-tournament culture: we'll help you find the right division
  • Charity components that give the island something back
  • Events on courts we build and maintain ourselves
