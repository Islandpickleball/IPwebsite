The trip where your game comes along

Small groups. Real coaching. Great courts in places worth traveling to — and good company the whole way. Our first escapes are taking shape now.

What we're exploring

A winter desert escape — think warm-weather courts, morning clinics, and round robins under the sun. Full itinerary, pricing, and partners confirmed before bookings open.

How it will work

Coaching built into the trip: skills sessions in the morning, structured match play after, and a coach on court with you — the Three-and-Me approach, on the road.

Who it's for

Players who want more than court time — a hosted experience with the island crew's standards: organized, welcoming, and competitive without taking itself too seriously.
