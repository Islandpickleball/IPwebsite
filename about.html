About

Built around the game

David Kane — court builder, PPR Certified Coach, and competitor. The story behind Island Pickleball.

Meet David Kane

The builder who coaches

Island Pickleball started the way most island things do — with a general contractor's skill set, a love of the game, and a community that needed both.

David builds and refreshes courts across the San Juan Islands, coaches players from their first rally to tournament finals, and competes himself across Washington, Oregon, California, and British Columbia — from island club events to the PPA Tour, with regular podium finishes in mixed and men's doubles.

The through-line is simple: he understands both the surface and the player, and the space where the two meet is where the game gets good.

On the road

Competing, coaching, connected

From island club events to the PPA Tour — regular podium finishes in mixed and men's doubles, and a few photos with the best in the game.

Community

Rooted in the islands

Island Pickleball gives back to the courts and kids that make this place special.

School court lines

David donated painted pickleball lines on the local elementary school court — supporting youth play at recess, in PE, and after school. It's also where the Academy pipeline begins.

United Way benefit tournament

Organized and ran an island tournament benefiting United Way of San Juan County — competition in service of community.

Community court fund

Use code CRIslandPickleball at Pickleball Central for 5% off your order — and 5% comes back to support island court work.

The legacy we're building

People others are glad to play with

Our goal is bigger than developing good pickleball players. We want to develop people who are competitive without arrogance, confident without boasting, and composed without losing their joy — players who give everything to the game without taking anything away from the people around them.
