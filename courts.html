Island Pickleball Courts

Better play starts with the court

New builds, resurfacing, striping, and repairs for private, club, and community courts across the San Juan Islands.

What we build

Full-scope court services

From a backyard single court to a full club resurfacing — layout, surfacing, color coats, lines, posts, and nets, done right.

New court builds

Layout, base preparation, acrylic surfacing, posts, and nets for private and club courts — built for how the game is actually played.

Resurfacing

Cleaning, crack repair, and fresh acrylic color coats that make a tired court play — and look — new again.

Line striping

New pickleball lines, tennis-to-pickleball conversions, and dual-lined courts so one surface serves both games.

Repairs & maintenance

Crack repair, surface cleaning, and net replacement to protect your court through island winters.

Private courts

Lessons and sessions at your home court by arrangement — we can build it, stripe it, and coach on it.

Community courts

Club and community projects — including donated school court lines that got island kids playing at recess.

Recent work

Courts we've built and refreshed

Real projects across the islands — resurfacing, striping, and private court builds, built to hold up to island weather.

Brent's court — peeled, patchy, and past its prime; stripped, resurfaced, and re-lined to play like new.

Corson court — crack repair and resurfacing, finished with both tennis and pickleball lines on one surface.

Built for island evenings

Play doesn't stop at sunset

Long summer light, rainy winters, short days — island schedules don't fit daylight hours. We build courts with lighting, drainage, and surfaces chosen for the Pacific Northwest, so the game keeps going year-round.
