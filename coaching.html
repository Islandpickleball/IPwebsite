Real games, live coaching between points. You play with a coach as your partner — strategy, positioning, and shot selection called out as it happens, then debriefed between games.

Philosophy

One ball at a time

Coaching at Island Pickleball is built on mindset and partnership: play the ball in front of you, let the last point go, and keep communicating with your partner.

We work on strategy, shot selection, mechanics, footwork, and court awareness — but the foundation is the mental game, because that's what holds up when the score gets tight.
