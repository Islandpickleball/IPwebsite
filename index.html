01 / Coaches

Two coaches, one partnership.

Island Pickleball was founded by David Kane and Stephanie Levasheff, who compete together from Canada to California and coach together on the islands. Both are Professional Pickleball Registry (PPR) Certified Coaches and USA Pickleball Association members — so the instruction comes from two people still under the same competitive pressure you're learning to handle.

David Kane

Co-Founder · PPR Certified Coach · USA Pickleball Member

David coaches players from first-time paddle to tournament-ready, on public island courts and on private courts by arrangement. He competes across the Pacific Northwest and the national circuit, with frequent podium finishes in mixed and men's doubles. His teaching leans on strategy, shot selection, and the mental discipline of playing one ball at a time.

Stephanie Levasheff

Co-Founder · PPR Certified Coach · USA Pickleball Member

Stephanie plays mixed doubles with David at tournaments from Canada to California and coaches players of every level at Island Pickleball. She focuses on clean mechanics, court movement, and the partner communication habits that turn two individuals into a team that can win close games.

04 / Philosophy

Most points are lost by a busy mind, not a bad forehand.

We coach two things above all: playing in the now — one ball, one decision, no scoreboard in your head — and partner communication, the small habits of calling, covering, and resetting together that turn two players into a team.

Let the last point go. Talk to your partner. Play the ball in front of you.

Island Pickleball — how we coach

Pickleball Mindset

The mental side of the game as it actually plays out on a pickleball court — pressure, momentum, and the partnership.

The Inner Game of Tennis

Timothy Gallwey's classic: quiet the self-critic, trust trained mechanics, and let awareness rather than instruction do the correcting.

Technique still matters

We drill mechanics, footwork, and shot selection. But mindset is what holds up at 9-9.
