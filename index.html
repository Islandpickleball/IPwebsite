01 / Your coach

Coached by someone still competing.

Island Pickleball was founded by David Kane, a Professional Pickleball Registry (PPR) Certified Coach and USA Pickleball Association member. He competes from Canada to California and coaches on the islands — so the instruction comes from someone still under the same competitive pressure you're learning to handle.

David Kane

Founder · PPR Certified Coach · USA Pickleball Member

David coaches players from first-time paddle to tournament-ready, on public island courts and on private courts by arrangement. He competes across the Pacific Northwest and on the national circuit, with frequent podium finishes in mixed and men's doubles.

His teaching leans on strategy, shot selection, and the mental discipline of playing one ball at a time — plus the partner communication habits that turn two individuals into a team that can win close games.

03 / Philosophy

Most points are lost by a busy mind, not a bad forehand.

We coach two things above all: playing in the now — one ball, one decision, no scoreboard in your head — and partner communication, the small habits of calling, covering, and resetting together that turn two players into a team.

Let the last point go. Talk to your partner. Play the ball in front of you.

Island Pickleball — how we coach

Pickleball Mindset

The mental side of the game as it actually plays out on a pickleball court — pressure, momentum, and the partnership.

The Inner Game of Tennis

Timothy Gallwey's classic: quiet the self-critic, trust trained mechanics, and let awareness rather than instruction do the correcting.

Technique still matters

We drill mechanics, footwork, and shot selection. But mindset is what holds up at 9-9.
