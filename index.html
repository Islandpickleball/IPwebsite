The Island Pickleball way

Play hard. Be kind.

We develop people others are glad to share a court with — competitive without arrogance, confident without boasting, composed without losing their joy. That goes for our youngest Academy players and our most seasoned competitors alike.

Built around the game

The coach who builds the court, too

Most pickleball coaches show up to a court someone else built. Island Pickleball starts earlier — designing, surfacing, and striping the places the islands play on, then coaching the people who play there.

That connection between the surface and the player is the heart of everything we do: courts built for better play, coaching built around your progress, and experiences built around good company.
