How We Coach

Most points are not lost to a bad forehand. They are lost to a busy mind and a quiet partnership. So we coach two things above all: playing in the now — one ball, one decision, no scoreboard in your head — and partner communication, the small habits of calling, covering, and resetting together that turn two players into a team.

Let the last point go. Talk to your partner. Play the ball in front of you.

The approach draws on the mental side of the game — the same ground covered by Pickleball Mindset and Timothy Gallwey's The Inner Game of Tennis: quiet the self-critic, trust trained mechanics, and let awareness rather than instruction do the correcting. Technique still matters, and we drill it. But mindset is what holds up at 9-9.
