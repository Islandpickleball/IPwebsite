Where Donations Go

Elementary school sport court

Surface repair, resurfacing, striping, nets, and paddles so island kids have a free, well-maintained court at school.

Lesson scholarship program

Need-based scholarships covering youth camp fees and adult instruction, so cost never decides who gets to learn.

Pickleball advocacy

Working with parks, schools, and the county on new and shared public courts, permits, lighting, and open play hours.

Where it's needed most

Undesignated gifts get pooled and applied to whichever project is closest to being funded.
